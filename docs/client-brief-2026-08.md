# **Strategic Marketer Website Direction**

## **Creative Brief & Homepage Strategy**

### **Version 1 – Executive Direction**

# **Project Objective**

The Strategic Marketer website is no longer intended to represent a traditional marketing agency or a software company.

Its purpose is to establish Strategic Marketer as the trusted authority that helps businesses identify, install, and activate AI systems that produce real business outcomes.

**The website is not intended to sell a specific product such as Content Velocity or IdeoLab.**

Instead, the website should communicate our philosophy, our credibility, and our methodology, then move visitors toward scheduling an AI Business Assessment where the appropriate solution is recommended.

The website should feel modern, authoritative, intelligent, and consultative.

The desired feeling is:

*"These people understand the real problem with AI, and they know how to solve it."*

---

# **Primary Audience**

Primary

• Business owners  
• Executives  
• Professionals  
• Growing companies

Secondary

• Agencies (separate navigation path)

The homepage should be written almost entirely for business owners.

Agency messaging should exist as a separate section of the website and should not distract from the primary experience.

---

# **Primary Conversion Goal**

**Schedule an AI Business Assessment**

The consultation becomes the mechanism through which Strategic Marketer determines the appropriate solution for the business.

The homepage should never force visitors to choose products.

It should guide them toward the consultation.

---

# **Secondary Conversion Goal**

**Exit-intent popup**

If a visitor attempts to leave the site without booking:

Offer a free AI workshop or educational training.

Goal: Capture the lead before they leave.

---

# **Brand Positioning**

Strategic Marketer is not positioned as:

• an AI agency  
• an AI marketing company  
• an AI software company  
• an AI operating system

Instead...

Strategic Marketer is positioned as the company that helps businesses install AI correctly.

The messaging should revolve around implementation rather than information.

This becomes the heartbeat of the homepage.

AI has incredible potential.

Instead they:

# **Strategic Marketer Website Concept**

This is not a traditional marketing agency website and it should not look like a generic AI SaaS website. The homepage should feel like the natural continuation of our highest-performing Facebook ads.

## **CRITICAL VISUAL DIRECTION**

Use the attached/reference Strategic Marketer Facebook ads as the primary visual design language for this website.

Study and carry forward their:

* Typography  
* Font weight and hierarchy  
* Color palette  
* Contrast  
* Graphic treatments  
* Background treatments  
* Image style  
* Use of faces/people if applicable  
* Iconography  
* Shapes  
* Borders  
* Cards  
* Callout boxes  
* Highlight treatments  
* Overall level of simplicity  
* Direct-response energy  
* Visual pacing  
* Amount of copy  
* Headline styling  
* CTA button styling

Do **not** take the ad and simply stretch it into a webpage.

**“Yes, this is clearly the same company and the same conversation I just clicked on.”**

Avoid creating visual dissonance between the ad and landing experience.

# **WEBSITE OBJECTIVE**

The Strategic Marketer website exists primarily to:

1. Establish authority and credibility.  
2. Explain our point of view about AI.  
3. Help business owners recognize why their current approach to AI is not producing meaningful results.  
4. Introduce our proprietary methodology.  
5. Demonstrate proof and experience.  
6. Encourage qualified business owners to schedule an **AI Business Assessment**.

This is primarily an **authority \+ consultation website**, not an ecommerce website and not a traditional product sales page.

The homepage should still be built using strong direct-response principles because a meaningful percentage of visitors will be arriving from cold Facebook traffic.

---

# **PRIMARY AUDIENCE**

Design and write primarily for:

* Business owners  
* Founders  
* Executives  
* Professionals  
* Established small and midsize businesses

These visitors may know very little about Strategic Marketer.

Assume cold traffic.

They need to quickly understand:

* Who we are  
* What we believe  
* What problem we solve  
* Why our approach is different  
* Why they should trust us  
* What they should do next

Agencies are a secondary audience and should have a separate navigation path labeled something like:

**For Agencies**

Do not make agencies part of the primary homepage narrative.

---

# **IMPORTANT BRAND POSITIONING**

Strategic Marketer should NOT be positioned as:

* A traditional digital marketing agency  
* An AI marketing agency  
* Just a software company  
* An AI operating system  
* A content company  
* A collection of individual products

Do not make **Content Velocity** or **IdeoLab** the centerpiece of this homepage.

Strategic Marketer serves businesses directly while also licensing technology and systems to agencies.

Publicly presenting individual products too aggressively can create channel confusion.

Instead, position Strategic Marketer as an experienced company that helps businesses:

**Identify, install and activate the right AI systems around the way their business actually works.**

The website sells the **approach, authority and assessment**.

Specific products or solutions can be prescribed later based on the business.

## **Stop experimenting with AI. Start putting it to work inside your business.**

Strategic Marketer helps businesses move from:

* watching AI videos  
* buying courses

* saving prompts

* feeling overwhelmed

to actually having useful AI systems installed and activated inside the company.

# **HOMEPAGE STRUCTURE**

## **SECTION 1 — HERO**

The hero must immediately establish relevance for cold traffic.

Explore headline territory such as:

**Stop Experimenting With AI. Start Putting It to Work Inside Your Business.**

Supporting copy should communicate:

Strategic Marketer helps established businesses identify, install and activate practical AI systems built around their brand, knowledge, customers and growth priorities.

Primary CTA:

**Schedule Your AI Business Assessment**

Possible secondary CTA:

**See How Our Approach Works**

Do not create several competing CTAs.

The assessment should remain the primary conversion action.

The hero should feel bold and direct like a high-performing Facebook ad but elevated enough for an established business brand.

---

## **SECTION 2 — AUTHORITY / TRUST BAR**

Cold visitors need immediate credibility.

Create a visually strong but clean credibility section using verified company proof such as:

* Twice recognized by Inc. Magazine among America's fastest-growing companies  
* 15+ years in software, marketing technology and business systems  
* Thousands of businesses trained, coached or supported  
* Longstanding experience helping businesses and agencies use technology to grow

The intended takeaway:

**Strategic Marketer was building business technology long before AI became the latest trend.**

Do not make this feel like a trophy case.

Make it reinforce why Strategic Marketer is qualified to guide a business through AI transformation.

---

## **SECTION 3 — THE AI PROBLEM**

Create a highly relatable section around the current experience of business owners.

Possible headline territory:

**The Problem Isn't Access to AI. It's Knowing What to Actually Do With It.**

Illustrate the cycle:

Watch another YouTube video.

Download another prompt.

Buy another course.

Subscribe to another tool.

Experiment.

Get distracted.

Start over.

Nothing meaningful gets installed.

This should create emotional recognition without becoming negative or fear-based.

The visitor should think:

**“That is exactly what has been happening.”**

---

## **SECTION 4 — DATA / MARKET PROOF**

Use credible statistics to reinforce the business case.

Leave placeholders where final sourced statistics need to be inserted.

Categories should include:

* Business adoption of AI  
* AI investment  
* Productivity potential  
* Competitive advantage  
* Failure to move AI pilots into implementation  
* Employee or business-owner overwhelm  
* Problems created by disconnected AI tools  
* Importance of trusted business data/context

Design this as a modern visual statistics section rather than a wall of text.

Example format:

**XX%**  
of businesses are investing in AI...

**XX%**  
struggle to move from experimentation to implementation...

etc.

Do not fabricate statistics.

Use clearly labeled placeholders if exact statistics have not been provided.

---

## **SECTION 5 — OUR POINT OF VIEW**

Transition from the market problem into Strategic Marketer's unique belief.

Possible headline:

**Before AI Can Work for Your Business, It Has to Understand Your Business.**

Explain in simple language that AI without context is essentially working from incomplete information.

Introduce the importance of:

* Brand identity  
* Voice  
* Knowledge  
* Customers  
* Positioning  
* Approved facts  
* Source of truth

Lead directly into the methodology visualization.

## **SECTION 7 — FROM INTELLIGENCE TO ACTIVATION**

* Build consistent brand visibility

* Automate repetitive processes  
* Centralize business knowledge  
* Improve team productivity

---

## **SECTION 8 — HOW THE ASSESSMENT WORKS**

Explain what happens when a business schedules an AI Business Assessment.

Keep it simple and consultative.

Suggested process:

### **1\. Understand**

Review the business, goals, current systems and AI usage.

### **2\. Diagnose**

Identify gaps, bottlenecks and missing foundational intelligence.

### **3\. Prioritize**

Determine where AI can create the greatest near-term business value.

### **4\. Recommend**

Develop the right combination of technology, implementation, automation, training or managed support.

### **5\. Activate**

Create a practical roadmap to move from experimentation to implementation.

CTA:

**Schedule Your AI Business Assessment**

Make the assessment feel valuable on its own.

Do not make it look like a disguised software demo.

* Human

* Business-focused  
* Conversational

* Specific

# **DIRECT-RESPONSE REQUIREMENTS**

Although this is an authority website rather than a traditional landing page, apply strong conversion principles throughout.

The page should contain:

* Strong message match from Facebook ads  
* Immediate relevance  
* Clear headline  
* Early credibility  
* Problem recognition  
* Differentiated mechanism  
* Proprietary methodology  
* Data-supported claims  
* Strong social proof  
* Business breadth  
* Risk reduction  
* Repeated primary CTA  
* Clear next step  
* Exit-intent lead capture  
* Mobile-first design  
* Fast visual comprehension  
* Short paragraphs  
* Strong visual hierarchy  
* Scannable sections

Do not make the website so “beautiful” that the visitor has to work to understand what Strategic Marketer actually does.

**Clarity \> cleverness.**

**Credibility \> hype.**

**Conversion \> decoration.**

---

# **MOBILE EXPERIENCE**

Assume a substantial percentage of Facebook traffic will arrive on mobile.

Design mobile intentionally rather than simply stacking desktop elements.

On mobile:

* Hero headline must remain easy to consume  
* CTA should appear quickly  
* Methodology graphic must remain understandable  
* Proof should be easy to swipe/scan  
* Typography should be large and readable  
* Avoid oversized sections  
* Avoid excessive animation  
* Preserve page speed  
* Consider a persistent mobile CTA for booking the assessment

---

# **OVERALL EXPERIENCE**

The final concept should feel like a combination of:

**Direct-response clarity \+ established business authority \+ modern technology company sophistication.**

Do not make Strategic Marketer look like a newly created AI startup.

Do not make it look like a legacy digital agency either.

The company should feel established, sophisticated and current.

The visitor should leave with five beliefs:

1. **Strategic Marketer understands why businesses are struggling to implement AI.**  
2. **They have a distinct methodology rather than just another collection of tools.**  
3. **They have enough history and proof that I can trust them.**  
4. **They can help determine what AI should actually look like inside my company.**  
5. **Scheduling an AI Business Assessment is the logical next step.**

Use the referenced high-performing Facebook ads as the **visual source of truth**, and build the website experience outward from that established creative language.
